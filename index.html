Gaji Bags

The Everything Bag of Taiwan

Durable, Affordable & Practical

Gaji bags—traditional Taiwanese shopping bags—are colorful woven nylon bags commonly used for shopping or as an everyday carry-all. They are also known affectionately as "grandma bags" because older women often carried them when shopping at local markets.

What is a Gaji Bag?

Gaji bags are traditional Taiwanese shopping bags made from colorful woven nylon mesh. Extremely durable, affordable, and practical, they have long served as a household essential across Taiwan.

Also fondly referred to as "grandma bags," this nickname stems from the past when older women frequently carried them while grocery shopping at local traditional markets.

The Story & History

Japanese Colonial Period
Origins in Jingliao, Tainan

Gaji bags were first made in the rural village of Jingliao in northern Tainan during the Japanese colonial period. The original bags were crafted by hand using natural plants.

1960s
Nylon & Machine Production

During Taiwan's industrialization in the 1960s, manufacturers began producing them with red, blue, and green nylon. Around the same time, machines were employed to manufacture the bags, making them durable, affordable, and practical—quickly turning them into a household essential.

1980s
Two-Decade Decline

When the economy improved in the 1980s, the bags fell out of favor for about two decades. With more money on hand, people turned to newer and more fashionable products.

Present Day
The Vintage Fashion Comeback

Today, with vintage fashion back in style, Gaji bags have made a strong comeback and are more popular than ever before. They have even been adapted into a wide variety of modern designs.

Adapted Modern Designs

Fanny Packs

Reimagined into trendy waist bags for hands-free urban exploration and daily activities.

Backpacks

Designed into casual backpacks, blending nostalgic woven mesh with ergonomic utility.

Coin Purses

Crafted into compact, charming coin purses—a perfect everyday vintage accessory.

“For the older generation, Gaji bags bring back fond memories; for the younger generation, they serve as an important connection to the past.”
