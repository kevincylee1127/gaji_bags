What is a Gaji Bag?

Gaji bag (茄芷袋, Ga-Ji de-ya) is a traditional, durable, and lightweight Taiwanese market tote bag featuring a distinctive red, green, blue, and white striped nylon mesh pattern.

Originally born as a practical bag for daily errands, farming, and grocery shopping, these bags have transformed their humble working-class origins to become a globally recognized symbol of Taiwanese heritage with a retro-chic design.

History and Origins

1895 - 1945
First Reveal

The Gaji bag originated during the Japanese colonial period in a rural village of Taiwan, where local farmers hand-wove soft rush grass to make bags for farming and earn extra household income. The name "Ga-Ji" (茄芷）is believed to derive from the Japanese word for crochet or weaving (かぎ編み Kagiami).

1960s
Industrial Shift

During Taiwan's industrialization in the 1960s, production shifted from hand-woven rush grass to machine-stitched, heavy-duty nylon mesh. The signature tricolor: red, green, and blue emerged, making the bag lightweight, durable, washable, quick-drying, and reuseable for everyday life.

Present Day
A Modern Fashion & Cultural Icon

Today, Gaji bags are embraced by old and young generations. Affectionately nicknamed as the "Taiwan LV" (Louis Vuitton, but MUCH cheaper!) for its iconic status and everyday utility. It became a household staple used by generations and has transitioned from a humble utility item into a symbol of Taiwanese culture.
